## Exploration bounds and exit

Question: can installed app-server configuration/capability inspection and retained
protocol regressions detect September integration failures without model turns?
Smallest probe: production validators via sanitised event/submission fixtures, then
initialise/configuration/capability inspection in a synthetic workspace with no
turn/start. Evidence owner: runtime preflight receipts and regression tests.
Exit: representative regressions pass and effective local profile is verified.
Generated content and journal growth remain live uncertainties, covered by live
bounds and the final complete fixture.

Bootstrap allowance before ledger adoption: **zero live model probes or complete
attempts**. No-model app-server inspection is permitted. Already consumed live
activity is therefore zero; ledger adoption must retain that explicit observation.
Selected finite policy: three complete attempts, two live focused probes, 750
reserved contexts and 240 protected for final qualification. Each broker caps
observed contexts at three; reservations include two concurrent-child slack.
Both fixture outcomes retain the unchanged 24-turn profile. Detailed semantics
belong in the qualification controls guide.
Do not spend the final complete-attempt headroom on probes. Two consecutive broad
failures enter diagnosis; unchanged failures are suppressed immediately. Repairs
must pass a relevant focused probe before another broad attempt.
